Close the lid. Carry on.

Codex or Claude Code is still working. Your build or download isn't finished. But you need to go.

LidLid keeps your MacBook awake with the lid closed so local tasks can keep running as you move. No charger or external display needed. Reopen your MacBook to restore normal sleep behavior. Switch it on again for your next closed-lid session.

Small controls for work in progress
• For coding tasks, builds, renders and downloads.
• Set 30 minutes to 8 hours, or keep going until you reopen. Ends on reopening or at your limit, whichever comes first.
• Open-lid keep-awake and Keep display awake are always free.
• Closed-lid mode ends at 10% battery while on battery, or when macOS reports serious thermal pressure.
• Optional launch at login. English and Simplified Chinese, with light and dark appearance.
• No account, ads or tracking. No reading your code or chats. Preferences and diagnostics stay on your Mac.

Before you start
Designed for MacBooks running macOS 14+ (Apple Silicon and Intel). Full closed-lid mode needs the separately installed, free LidLid Compatibility component and approval through macOS. Open-lid mode needs no component. Disconnect external displays before using closed-lid mode. Online tasks still need connectivity. Turn off closed-lid mode before choosing Sleep.
