Your work is still running. You need to go.

LidLid is a small menu bar app for MacBooks. Switch it on before closing your laptop to keep local work running as you move to another spot. No more balancing an open laptop while you walk.

One session for this lid close
Turn it on when you need it. Reopening the lid ends the session automatically. Turn it on again for the next time you need it.

No charger. No external display.
Keep working on battery power as you move between desks or find a new place to sit. An external monitor is not required.

Keep the work moving
Useful for coding tasks, builds, tests, renders and downloads, including work running in Codex or Claude Code. LidLid manages your Mac's sleep state; it does not read your code, control AI tools or operate your tasks. Online work still needs a network connection.

Set a boundary for this session
Choose 30 minutes, 1, 2, 4 or 8 hours, or keep the session until you reopen the lid. With a time limit, the session ends when you reopen the lid or reach the limit, whichever comes first.

Small controls. Clear state.
• A guardian in your menu bar: open eyes when on, closed eyes when off
• Ends at 10% battery while running on battery power
• Ends when macOS reports serious or critical thermal pressure
• Optional launch at login
• English and Simplified Chinese
• No account, advertising or tracking; preferences and diagnostic records stay on your Mac

Getting started
1. Open the LidLid icon in your menu bar.
2. Switch on and wait for On.
3. Close the lid. Reopening ends the session.

Compatibility and use
Requires an Apple Silicon MacBook and macOS 14 or later. Intel Macs and desktop Macs are not supported. Disconnect external displays before enabling a session.

Keep your Mac ventilated. End the session before putting it in a bag or sleeve. LidLid does not make online tasks work offline, or save and manage work in other apps.
