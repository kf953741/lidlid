LidLid Privacy Policy

Updated November 1, 2026

LidLid is a Mac menu bar utility developed by Fei Kang. The app runs locally, requires no account, does not upload usage data to the developer, and includes no advertising or tracking SDK.

Information handled on your Mac
LidLid stores your language and time-limit preferences. It reads lid state, battery level, power source, system thermal state and elapsed time to manage the session you enable. It also checks whether the potentially conflicting Clamshell lid-control app is running.

Local diagnostic records describe activation, lid events, ending, restoration, errors and identifiers of LidLid’s own processes. LidLid does not read your projects, code, file contents, AI conversations or browsing history.

No automatic uploads
The app has no account system, advertising, analytics or developer-operated network service. It does not automatically upload preferences, diagnostic records or device state. It does not use this information to identify you or track you across apps or websites.

Local retention and deletion
Preferences remain until you change or remove them. Diagnostic records stay on your Mac. When a record file reaches its size limit, older records are archived locally; archives are not uploaded automatically and have no automatic deletion period.

To remove all local preferences and records from the sandboxed app, quit LidLid first, then use Finder’s Go to Folder to locate and remove ~/Library/Containers/dev.kf.lidonce/. Earlier non-sandboxed versions may have left records in ~/Library/Application Support/LidOnce/ and preferences in ~/Library/Preferences/dev.kf.lidonce.plist. Deleting the app alone may not remove these files.

Website and external links
Choosing a privacy or support link in the app opens the relevant page in your browser. This website has no visitor analytics SDK, marketing cookies or sign-in. Vercel hosts the website and processes IP addresses, browser details and requests for delivery, operation and security. See the Vercel Privacy Notice (https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice). Website request processing is separate from the information the app stores locally.

Downloads and voluntary support requests
Current DMG downloads are provided through GitHub and are subject to the GitHub Privacy Statement (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). If you download or purchase through the App Store, Apple handles that transaction under its applicable terms.

If you choose to email us, we use the contact address, message, Mac model, macOS version, log or screenshot you provide to help address your request. Email service providers also process transmission and storage under their own policies. Send only information relevant to your request, without passwords, keys or unrelated private content. You can use the same email address to request correction or deletion of support content within our control. Third-party processing and retention are governed by those services’ own policies.

Contact and updates
For support or privacy questions, email developer Fei Kang at kf953741@gmail.com (mailto:kf953741@gmail.com). The LidLid support page (https://lidlid.vercel.app/en/support/) also provides usage help. If a future version changes how information is handled, we will update this policy and the corresponding App Store privacy disclosures.
